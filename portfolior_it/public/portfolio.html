TheOU: Anonymous Campus Platform

Anonymous-by-default platform for a campus community.

The problem

Campus members needed a dedicated place to share stories and ask questions without revealing who they are.

Decision & solution

Built a campus-only platform where every request goes through one API that returns only public fields, and PostgreSQL enforces consistency, so anonymity and counts hold up under retries and concurrent votes.

What I delivered

  • Verified @ou.edu.vn sign-up with one-time OTP
  • Anonymous or nickname posts, comments, and upvotes
  • Private image uploads, owner delete, and admin moderation with an audit trail

Result

Retries to rows100 → 1
Vote count drift0
Automated tests406

VaniOAK: Log Monitoring & Alerting

Real-time log monitoring and alerting for Dev/Test.

The problem

Dev/Test logs were spread across services, with no single place to search, watch, or alert on them.

Decision & solution

Built an event-driven modular monolith: RabbitMQ decouples ingestion from processing, Elasticsearch stores logs for search, and Redis deduplicates alerts before they reach Telegram.

What I delivered

  • Event-driven log pipeline with RabbitMQ and Elasticsearch
  • Real-time dashboards over Server-Sent Events
  • Telegram alerts with Redis-based deduplication

Result

Logs per second1,000+
Success rate99%
End-to-end latency~2s
